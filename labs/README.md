## How the labs work
- **One app all day: OctoCAT Supply.** It has a React website, an Express/TypeScript API and a SQLite database.
- **You work on the `workshop` branch.** A few Copilot files were removed from it on purpose, and in the labs you put them back.
- **The `main` branch is the original app: your answer key.** Some labs end with a *Compare* step.
- **Each session follows the same rhythm:** a short talk, then a hands-on lab, then a wrap-up.
- **Work in mixed pairs** where you can, for example a backend developer with a platform engineer.
- **Copilot is probabilistic.** Your answers will differ from your neighbour's, and that's expected.
- **You don't need to be a TypeScript expert.** Copilot writes the code; your job is to describe, review and verify.

## Conventions in the lab sheets
- Text in grey boxes is meant to be copied. Replace anything in `<angle brackets>`.
- **Ask**, **Agent** and **Plan** are the choices in the agent picker at the bottom of the Chat view.
- "New chat" means the **+** at the top of the Chat view.
- ⏱ marks the suggested time for each part.
